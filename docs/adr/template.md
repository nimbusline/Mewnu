# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

- **Status:** proposed
- **Decision date:** YYYY-MM-DD or unknown
- **Documented:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** participants

## Context

Describe one decision problem, its product goal, and relevant constraints. Separate independently changeable choices into their own records.

## Decision

State the concrete choice, scope, and verifiable rules.

## Alternatives

Compare viable alternatives addressing the same problem. Explain their benefits, costs, and why the selected choice fits the stated constraints. Use the present tense. Document historical discussions or approvals only when supported by evidence.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, and observable effects on users, implementation, operation, and maintenance. Include residual responsibilities such as credential renewal or integration checks. Keep these effects distinct from acceptance criteria.

## Implementation and validation

Provide observable acceptance criteria and implementation references. Keep incidental code mechanics in source references unless they are necessary to preserve the decision. Distinguish existing test evidence from checks required for future changes. Exclude conversation transcripts, personal details, calendar content, and credentials. Link any superseded ADR.
